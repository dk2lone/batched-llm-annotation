# Full existing-dataset grid without an accuracy gate

Decision recorded 2026-09-14 02:18:49 UTC, before any X1R4 joint output.
The research question is the difference between joint, exact-context, and solo
annotation on the same texts. Solo accuracy is not required to define that
relative contrast. The X1R4 capability floors were a choice to focus its
primary claim on useful annotators; applying them to the entire study would
prevent the requested four-model × four-dataset comparison.

The active plan therefore adds a separate complete grid of Qwen, Granite,
Llama, and Mistral across Civil Comments, MultiNLI, IMDb, and AG News. All
sixteen cells are included irrespective of the A6 accuracy or parse-rate
floor. The seven cells already admitted in X1R4 keep their original sealed
analysis, call schedules, C1 gate, and B4 cell selection. The nine other
cells require new pre-output call, outcome, analysis, and budget seals. This
record is planning only and does not authorize GPU execution.

For the full grid, use identical source texts within each cell for joint,
exact-context target-only, and solo annotation, plus fake-batch controls.
Prespecify item counts from solo-only information, call order, randomization,
malformed-output handling, covariance contrasts, cell and model/task margins,
interaction, multiplicity adjustment, and stopping rules before any joint
output. Report solo accuracy and parse rate as outcomes or moderators, with
all failures visible. Do not drop a cell because its relative result is null,
negative, or inconvenient. If a cell has no usable labels, report the
estimand as unidentifiable; if errors have no variation, report that limit on
what the cell can show.

The new full-grid result may test whether the relative batching effect
generalizes across the four existing tasks and four fixed models. It does not
retroactively replace the original seven-cell X1R4 claim or erase its failed
screen. Its prospective seal must be completed before A8 reveals any new
joint result, even if the nine additional cells are run after A8. The existing
80-hour X1R4 stop and 10-hour reserve are unchanged; the added work needs a
separately verified budget. The A12 independent-dataset grid remains later
replication, but no longer uses an all-four-pass solo accuracy gate.

The historical `20260914T014726Z-crossed-4x4-plan.md` and
`20260914T015953Z-a12-expansion-order.md` are retained. Their all-four-pass
eligibility rule is superseded for new planning; their scientific and budget
seals were never created.
